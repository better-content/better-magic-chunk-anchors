## Primary shipped gameplay promise and acquisition

`AnchorRegistries` registers seven placeable block/item pairs in `better_magic_chunk_anchors`: flux, kinetic, source, lifeforce, pressure, soul and spirit chunk anchors. A powered, non-redstone-disabled anchor requests **fully ticking** Forge block tickets for its centered 3×3 chunks; power starvation, redstone disable and removal release those tickets (`AnchorData`, `AnchorMath`, `AnchorLifecycleGameTests`). `AnchorWorkApi.supportingAnchor` additionally checks actual tickets and a live anchor, and declines work close to an online player; do not equate its operation-support decision with merely seeing an anchor block. Power comes respectively from FE, Create rotational speed, Ars Source, Blood Magic life essence, PneumaticCraft air, Goety soul energy or tagged Malum spirits (`ArcaneAnchorBlockEntity`, `KineticAnchorBlockEntity`, `AnchorConfig`). Defaults target two hours at 20 TPS, not an independently measured runtime guarantee. Ownership and restorable state are persisted through `AnchorSavedData`; the item tooltip reports stored charge (`AnchorBlockItem`). These are source behaviors, not claims about a verified installed candidate.

**Acquisition references:** the IDs and variant core-block references are enumerated in `AnchorVariant.java` (redstone block, raw gold block, source gem block, bloodstonebrick, compressed iron block, ominous stone, block of soulstone). Core-block lookup falls back to amethyst if a referenced block is absent; these references are *not* crafting recipes. `AnchorRegistries.java` registers each block item; `src/main/resources/data/better_magic_chunk_anchors/loot_tables/blocks/*_chunk_anchor.json` describes block drops. This repository has **no anchor crafting recipe JSON under `src/main/resources/data`**. Check the candidate pack's datapacks/scripts and recipe viewer for actual survival acquisition and recipe roots; do not present registration, creative/command access or a drop table as proof of a craftable progression path. Confirm all seven accessible to players without admin commands, or record the missing path as a progression blocker. No GUI screen is registered here: block interaction, tooltips, comparator output and `/` command implementation in `ChunkLoaderCommands` are the relevant interfaces; test pack-level GUI/recipe contracts separately rather than inventing a native anchor GUI.
